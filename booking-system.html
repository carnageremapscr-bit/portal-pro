FAQ

Quick answers.

Is BookedBro part of Portal Pro?

No. BookedBro is a separate booking service. Portal Pro is a partner that introduces it to appointment-led businesses.

Do my customers need an account or app?

No. They open your booking link, pick a service and time, and book from any device.

How much does it cost?

BookedBro sets its own plans and offers. See the BookedBro pricing page for current details.

Can I add it to my Portal Pro website?

Yes. Share your booking link from your website, VRM lookup page, socials and Google profile. Contact us and we'll help.

Can customers book a remap or dyno run?

Yes. You can set up services such as Stage 1 and Stage 2 remaps, dyno runs and diagnostics, each with its own duration, price and availability.
