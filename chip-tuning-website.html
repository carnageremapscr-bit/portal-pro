Chip Tuning Website FAQ

How much does a chip tuning website cost?

Our websites start from a one-time design fee that includes everything — design, coding, VRM lookup, remap calculator, and lead capture setup. The tuning tools run at £17.99/month for unlimited lookups sitewide. Contact us via WhatsApp for a custom quote.

What features are included?

Every tuning website includes: VRM reg lookup with DVLA integration, remap calculator with stage 1/2/3 gains, WhatsApp lead capture, mobile-responsive design, SEO optimisation with schema markup, Google Analytics, and a professional dark automotive theme.

Do you design for mobile remappers?

Absolutely. Many of our clients are mobile remappers who need a professional online presence. We build sites that showcase your coverage area, services, and pricing with built-in VRM lookup so customers can check their vehicle before contacting you.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We can build on platforms you can edit, or provide ongoing support where we handle all updates for you. Most tuning businesses prefer us to manage updates so they can focus on tuning.

How long does it take to build?

Most chip tuning websites are designed, built, and live within 7 days. Complex projects with custom portals may take slightly longer. We'll give you a clear timeline upfront.

Will my website rank on Google?

We build every site with SEO in mind — proper meta tags, schema markup, fast loading, mobile-first design, and local SEO signals. We also help with Google Business Profile optimisation and ongoing SEO support.
