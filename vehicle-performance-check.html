Performance Check FAQ

How does the vehicle performance check work?

Enter your UK vehicle registration and the system uses DVLA data to identify your exact vehicle — make, model, engine, year, and fuel type. It then shows you the available power gains from stage 1, 2, and 3 ECU remapping including BHP, torque, and percentage improvements.

Is the performance check free?

Yes, completely free for end users. No sign-up, no email required — just enter your reg and get instant results.

What vehicles are covered?

Most UK-registered cars, vans, and commercial vehicles. We cover 30+ manufacturers including BMW, Audi, VW, Ford, Mercedes, Vauxhall, Jaguar, Land Rover, Toyota, Nissan, and many more.

How accurate are the results?

Results are based on real dyno data from verified tuning files. Actual gains can vary by 5-10% depending on vehicle condition, mileage, fuel quality, and existing modifications.

Can I book a remap after checking my vehicle?

Absolutely. After seeing your gains, click the WhatsApp button to get in touch directly. We’ll confirm your vehicle details and arrange a booking.
