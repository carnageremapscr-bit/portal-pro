Stage 1 Search Tool FAQ

What is a stage 1 remap search tool?

It's a search widget for your tuning website. Customers enter their reg and instantly see the BHP and torque gains available from a stage 1 remap. Every search captures a lead for you.

How does it capture leads?

When a customer searches, you receive an instant WhatsApp message and email with their vehicle details. You know exactly what they're interested in before they even call.

Can I use my own tuning data?

Yes. We build the tool around your actual results — your BHP figures, your torque gains, your pricing. Not generic data.

How does VRM lookup work?

Customers enter their reg number and the system auto-detects their vehicle using DVLA data. It identifies the exact make, model and engine, then shows relevant stage 1 results instantly.

Can I show stage 2 and stage 3 results too?

Absolutely. The tool supports multiple stages, custom tuning options, and comparison views so customers can see all available upgrades.

How do I add it to my website?

We provide a single iframe embed code. Paste it into any page on your website — works with WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, and any other platform.
