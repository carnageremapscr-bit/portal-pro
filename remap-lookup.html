Remap Lookup FAQ

How does the remap lookup work?

Enter your vehicle reg and the system uses DVLA data to identify your exact vehicle — make, model, year, engine, and fuel type. It then matches this against a tuning database to show expected BHP and torque gains for stage 1, 2, and 3 remaps.

Is the remap lookup free?

Yes, looking up your vehicle's remap gains is completely free. No sign-up required.

Can I add this to my tuning website?

Yes. We provide embeddable remap lookup widgets for tuning businesses. Every customer lookup captures their details as a lead with WhatsApp and email alerts to you.

What vehicles does it cover?

Most UK-registered cars and vans, petrol and diesel, from BMW, Audi, VW, Ford, Mercedes, Vauxhall, Jaguar, Land Rover, and many more.

How accurate are the remap gains?

Results are based on real dyno data. Actual gains can vary by 5-10% depending on vehicle condition, fuel quality, and modifications. The lookup provides reliable baseline expectations.
