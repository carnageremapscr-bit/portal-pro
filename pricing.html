Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The only costs are the one-time setup fee and monthly subscription. Hosting, SSL, updates, support, and all features are included. Payment processing fees (Stripe/PayPal) are separate if you use those integrations.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, anytime. If you upgrade, we'll prorate the difference. If you downgrade, the change takes effect at your next billing cycle.

Is there a contract or minimum term?

No long-term contracts. Pay monthly and cancel anytime. We believe in earning your business every month through great service, not locking you in.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer, credit/debit cards (via Stripe), and PayPal for monthly subscriptions. Setup fees can be paid by any of these methods.

How long does setup take?

Starter plans typically take 3-5 business days. Professional plans take 1-2 weeks. Enterprise plans vary based on custom requirements (usually 2-4 weeks).

Do you offer a free trial?

We offer free demos and a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied within 30 days, we'll refund your setup fee-no questions asked.

Still have questions?

Chat with us on WhatsApp or email. We typically respond within 2 hours.
