VRM Widget Embed FAQ

How do I embed the VRM lookup on my website?

Simply copy and paste our iframe embed code into your website HTML. It works with WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, and any website that supports HTML.

What data does the VRM widget return?

The widget returns make, model, year, engine size, fuel type, BHP, emission class, MOT status, MOT expiry, tax status, keeper changes, insurance group, and more.

Does the VRM widget capture leads?

Yes. Every search triggers instant WhatsApp and email notifications with the customer's vehicle details, so you can follow up immediately.

Will it work on my WordPress site?

Yes. Add it using a Custom HTML block in the Gutenberg editor, an Elementor HTML widget, or paste directly into your theme template.

Can I customise the widget's appearance?

Yes. We can brand the widget to match your website's colours and style. Contact us to discuss customisation options.
