Remap Calculator FAQ

What is a remap calculator?

A remap calculator shows you the expected BHP and torque gains for your specific vehicle from ECU remapping. Enter your reg to see before/after figures for stage 1, 2 & 3 remaps.

How accurate is a remap calculator?

Results are based on real dyno data from thousands of remapped vehicles. Actual gains can vary by 5-10% depending on vehicle condition, fuel quality, and altitude.

Can I add a remap calculator to my tuning website?

Yes. We provide embeddable remap calculator widgets for tuning businesses. Customers search their vehicle, see gains, and you capture their details as leads.

Is the remap calculator free to use?

Yes, visitors can use it for free. For tuning businesses wanting this on their own website, contact us to discuss integration.

What vehicles does the calculator cover?

Most UK cars and vans — petrol and diesel — from BMW, Audi, VW, Ford, Mercedes, Vauxhall, Jaguar, Land Rover, and many more manufacturers.

Can it show stage 2 and stage 3 gains?

Yes. The calculator supports stage 1, 2, and 3 results with comparison views. Customers can see exactly what each stage offers for their vehicle.
